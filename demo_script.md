# EduFlow AI — 2-Minute Demo Script

---

## PRE-DEMO SETUP (do before recording)

- [ ] KB uploaded and fully processed (KG graph ready)
- [ ] At least 5+ topics with interactions so dashboard shows real data
- [ ] One question batch already generated (3-4 questions ready to show)
- [ ] One deep research report already completed
- [ ] One guided learning session already created with HTML lesson
- [ ] Browser at `localhost:3000/dashboard`, sidebar expanded
- [ ] Screen recording at 1080p or higher

---

## THE SCRIPT

### PART 1 — The Problem (0:00 - 0:15)

**[Screen: Dashboard visible in background, you speaking to camera or voiceover]**

> "Here's the problem with how students learn today. You finish a course, you get a grade — maybe 78 percent — and that's it. But that number tells you nothing. Which topics did you actually master? Which ones did you just get lucky on? Are you forgetting material you learned three weeks ago? And most dangerously — are you confident about topics you don't actually understand?"
>
> "We built EduFlow AI to solve exactly this."

---

### PART 2 — The Mastery Model (0:15 - 0:45)

**[Action: Point at the 4 stat cards at the top of the dashboard]**

> "Instead of a single grade, EduFlow tracks four independent signals for every topic a student studies."

**[Action: Click to expand a topic card — preferably one with an "Overconfident" or "Needs Work" badge]**

> "First — Bayesian Knowledge Tracing. This models the probability that a student has truly learned a concept, not just answered correctly. It accounts for lucky guesses and careless slips."
>
> "Second — engagement. Reading, chatting, problem-solving — all non-quiz activities contribute credit."
>
> "Third — retention. We apply the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve. If you haven't reviewed a topic in two weeks, your retention score drops — because the model knows you're likely forgetting."
>
> "And fourth — confidence calibration. We ask students to rate their confidence before each answer. Then we compare that against their actual accuracy."

**[Action: Point at the overconfident badge or signal breakdown bars]**

> "This student rated 4 out of 5 confidence but only got 50 percent correct. The system flags that as overconfidence — and that's the most dangerous gap in learning, because the student doesn't even know they need help."

---

### PART 3 — What the System Does With This (0:45 - 1:00)

**[Action: Scroll to the Activity Breakdown donut and Mastery Timeline chart]**

> "Every interaction is tracked — questions, problem-solving, research, chat — and the mastery timeline shows progression over time. This isn't a simple average. It's a cumulative Bayesian estimate that updates with every action."

**[Action: Scroll to Today's Study Plan section]**

> "Based on all four signals, the system generates a personalized study plan each day. Overconfident and declining topics get boosted to the top. Weak topics get guided lessons. Developing topics get quizzes. Strong topics get harder problems. The plan adapts as the student learns."

---

### PART 4 — The Learning Tools (1:00 - 1:40)

**[Action: Click "Knowledge Graph" in sidebar, let graph render]**

> "Now let me show you the tools. Every uploaded document is automatically parsed into a knowledge graph — concepts, methods, relationships, all extracted and connected."

**[Action: Type a search term, nodes highlight. Click a node to open sidebar panel.]**

> "Students can search any concept and see how it connects to the rest of their material. Clicking a node shows a synthesized description pulled from across their documents."

**[Action: Click "Question Generator" in sidebar, show pre-generated batch]**

> "The question generator uses three AI agents in a maker-checker pipeline — one generates questions from the knowledge base, another validates that each question is actually answerable from the student's own materials."

**[Action: Click a question, set confidence to 4/5, select an answer, click Submit]**

> "I'll answer this one... set my confidence... and submit."

**[Action: Point at the mastery delta toast that appears bottom-right]**

> "Immediately — the Bayesian model updates in real time. Mastery jumped from 42 to 48 percent. Every single answer shifts the model."

**[Action: Click "Smart Solver" in sidebar, show a completed solution]**

> "When students get stuck, the smart solver uses seven agents — an investigation loop searches the knowledge base and web for context, then a solve loop produces step-by-step reasoning with citations back to the source material. No hallucination — every claim is grounded in the student's actual documents."

---

### PART 5 — Deep Research & Guided Learning (1:40 - 1:55)

**[Action: Click "Deep Research" in sidebar, show a completed report]**

> "For deeper exploration, the research engine decomposes any topic into subtopics, searches across the knowledge base, web, and academic papers, then synthesizes a structured, cited report — from quick summaries to deep multi-source analysis."

**[Action: Click "Guided Learning" in sidebar, show a session with an HTML lesson]**

> "And for topics where the student needs real instruction, guided learning generates interactive step-by-step lessons directly from their own course content, with built-in Q&A."

---

### PART 6 — Closing (1:55 - 2:00)

**[Action: Click back to Dashboard]**

> "24 AI agents. Bayesian mastery modeling. Knowledge graphs. Forgetting curves. Confidence calibration. All running local-first — every piece of student data stays on their machine. Only LLM API calls leave the device."
>
> "This is EduFlow AI — because a grade should never be the end of understanding."
